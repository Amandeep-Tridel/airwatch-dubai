How the numbers are made

  • H₂S (Alphasense H2S-B4, A1 working / A2 auxiliary electrode): AAN 803 algorithm 1, [(A1−WE_e) − n_T·(A2−AE_e)] / sensitivity.
  • NH₃ (Alphasense NH3-B1, A0): 3-electrode conversion with H₂S cross-sensitivity compensation.
  • VOC (Alphasense PIDX-A-040, A3): fixed clean-air zero plus ideal-gas temperature/pressure correction, reported as isobutylene equivalent.
  • Values are despiked and smoothed with a Hampel + 1D Kalman filter, with live 1σ noise estimates reported alongside every reading.
